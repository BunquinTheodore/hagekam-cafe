Our story

Brewed where the sun meets the sea.

Our crest tells it best. A sunrise over Puerto Galera, palms on the shore, a woven basket and clay pot from the island makers, and two cups waiting on the table. Hagekam is a slow morning by the port, made for travelers stepping off the boat and neighbors who come back every day.

We opened our doors on July 13, 2026. Since then the menu has grown into a little island of its own: tiramisu lattes, crème brûlée drinks, croffles, pastas and silog plates.
